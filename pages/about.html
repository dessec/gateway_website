About Us

Gateway Metal Detectors is Hamilton's dedicated treasure hunting hub, founded on a lifelong passion for discovery. We carry professional-grade equipment from the world's leading brands — Nokta, Minelab, XP, and Garrett. Whether you're a first-time hobbyist or a seasoned detectorist, we have the gear and the knowledge to back it up. Serving Southern Ontario and shipping across Canada and worldwide.
