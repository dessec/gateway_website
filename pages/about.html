Meet Rick Desjardins: Hamilton's History Hunter

In an age of faceless online warehouses and automated chatbots, Rick Desjardins prefers to do business the old-fashioned way: face-to-face, handshake-to-handshake.

Rick isn’t just a retailer; he’s a lifelong detectorist. For years, he has been a rugged fixture at Haggler’s Flea Market (1565 Barton Street East), turning Sundays into Hamilton’s premier gathering hub for serious hobbyists and treasure hunters across Southern Ontario. He founded Gateway Metal Detectors with a simple, unshakeable belief: you shouldn't buy a machine you haven't held in your hands.

By completely rejecting the high-markup, shipping-heavy model of modern big-box websites, Rick operates strictly on a low-overhead, cash-and-carry basis. This old-school approach completely cuts out middleman processing fees and expensive shipping costs, allowing him to pass those direct savings straight back to the local community with incredibly fair pricing.

Whether you are looking for your very first detector or need a specialized replacement part, Rick is here to help you gear up for your next hunt. He doesn't just sell boxes; he provides a personal guarantee and the deep technical expertise that only comes from decades of digging in Ontario soil.

Stop by this Sunday—he's always ready to talk treasure.
