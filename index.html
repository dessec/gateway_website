Historical Chapter

St. Catharines Chapter · Founded 1980

Canadian Heritage Seekers

Non-Profit Heritage & Relic Hunting Club

A passionate organization of historical researchers and metal detectorists based in St. Catharines. Focused on documenting pioneer artifacts, coin shooting, and promoting public education on Ontario's rich local history.

GATEWAY Metal Detectors

ABOUT US: Gateway Metal Detectors is Hamilton's dedicated treasure hunting hub, founded on a lifelong passion for discovery. We carry professional-grade equipment from the world's leading brands — Nokta, Minelab, XP, and Garrett. Whether you're a first-time hobbyist or a seasoned detectorist, we have the gear and the knowledge to back it up. Serving Southern Ontario and shipping across Canada and worldwide.
